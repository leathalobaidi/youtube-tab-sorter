NAME:
YouTube Tab Sorter


SUMMARY:
Sort your YouTube tabs by video duration — shortest on the left, longest on the right. One click to organize your watch queue.


DESCRIPTION:
YouTube Tab Sorter organizes your browser tabs by video length with a single click.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HOW IT WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Click the extension icon in your toolbar
• All your YouTube video tabs instantly rearrange
• Shortest videos move to the left, longest to the right
• Non-YouTube tabs are moved to the right side

• Watching shorter videos first before longer content
• Organizing research or playlist tabs by length

PERMISSION JUSTIFICATIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TABS PERMISSION:
Required to query all tabs in the current window, read each tab's URL to identify which are YouTube video pages, and reorder tabs with chrome.tabs.move after sorting by video duration. The narrower "activeTab" permission is NOT sufficient: sorting must read the URLs of every tab in the window (not just the focused one) and must move non-active tabs, neither of which activeTab grants.


SCRIPTING PERMISSION:
Required to inject a one-shot function via chrome.scripting.executeScript into each YouTube video tab to read its video duration (from the HTML5 video element, the player time display, page metadata, or embedded page data). No persistent content script is bundled, and the injected function is part of the extension package — it is not fetched or executed from any remote source.


HOST PERMISSION (youtube.com):
Required to access YouTube video pages in order to extract video duration information for sorting. The extension only reads duration data and does not modify any page content.
